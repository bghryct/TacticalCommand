WW2 real-time tactics

Tactical Command — Normandy

Combined arms in the spirit of Close Combat, drawn at true scale: squads, tanks, guns, towns and rivers at their real sizes, with first-person control of any unit on the field. Seven campaigns (Normandy from both sides, Market Garden, the Eastern Front from both sides, North Africa and Italy, France 1940), ninety-six battlefields and a content editor.

Play Content editor
First load is heavy. The game downloads about 200 MB of scale models the first time, so tanks may show as plain shapes for a little while. Your browser keeps them after that. It plays best on a desktop browser such as Chrome, Edge or Firefox. Multiplayer needs the local relay server and is not available on this page.
